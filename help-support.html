Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account? +
Click the Register button, enter your full name, email, phone number and password, then submit your registration form.
How can I search for a house? +
Open the Search Homes page and enter your preferred location, price range and property type. Click Search Homes to view available properties.
How do I book a property? +
Select a property, open its details, choose your preferred visit date and click Confirm Booking.
How can I contact a property owner? +
Open the property details page and click Chat with Owner to start a conversation.
How do I report a suspicious property? +
Open the Report Fraud page, select the issue, describe the problem and submit your report.
What if I forget my password? +
Use the Forgot Password option on the Login page to start the password recovery process.
